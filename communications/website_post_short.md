Privacy-SATD Miner: first results

We ran a Germany-first OSS scan to surface privacy-related self-admitted technical debt. The pipeline collects PRs, issues, commits, and comments, then applies a keyword filter and a lightweight GDPR mapping.

Key results:
- 186 filtered instances across 35 repos
- Top keywords: tracking, encryption, consent
- GDPR mapping highlights Art.32, Art.6, Art.7

Images and the dashboard are ready. Full write-up and source links below.
